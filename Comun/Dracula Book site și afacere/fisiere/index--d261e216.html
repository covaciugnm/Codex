The House

A black rose, two dragons, one promise

Dracula Book was founded on a simple belief: that Transylvania — land of citadels, salt roads and long winters — still has stories the world has not heard. We write and publish them in English, German and Romanian, each series built by a dedicated editorial team and polished through multiple critical drafts before it ever reaches the press.

Our crest — a black rose guarded by two dragons — marks every spine we print. It is a promise of craft: no book leaves the house before it is the best version of itself.
